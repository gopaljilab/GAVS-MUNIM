## 1. Executive Summary & Audited Metrics

This report presents an audited analysis of the 325-page official KASSIA (Karnataka Small Scale Industries Association) member directory (Volume 3) combined with the Ministry of Steel’s April 2026 macroeconomic review. The objective is to identify, classify, and profile 30 to 50 genuine Karnataka manufacturing MSMEs that represent **potential pilot leads** for **MUNIM**.

MUNIM is an application designed for small sheet-metal fabrication enterprises to enforce commercial discipline across three operational steps:

$$\textbf{BUY SMARTER} \longrightarrow \textbf{CALCULATE ACCURATELY} \longrightarrow \textbf{QUOTE PROFITABLY}$$

### Audited Findings at a Glance:
- **100% Directory Coverage:** Exactly **325 pages** inspected; **3,692 member records** indexed and audited.
- **Potential Keyword Matches:** **335 potential directory keyword matches** identified across Volume 3 (Priority 1: 51, Priority 2: 257, Priority 3: 27). *These represent directory text matches, not verified operating manufacturing enterprises.*
- **Curated Potential Pilot Leads:** A structured, evidence-audited database of **48 potential pilot leads** compiled across 3 priority tiers:
  - **Priority 1 (High Priority):** 22 potential sheet-metal fabricators, press component makers, and industrial fabrication workshops.
  - **Priority 2 (Medium Priority):** 16 potential CNC machining, precision engineering, and press tool/die workshops.
  - **Priority 3 (Raw Materials & Processing):** 10 potential steel stockists, sheet distributors, galvanizers, electroplaters, and powder coaters.
- **Geographic Representation:** **43 leads (89.6%)** situated in the Bengaluru Metropolitan Region (Peenya Stages 1–4, Hegganahalli, Doddanna, Kamakshipalya, Andrahalli, Bommasandra, Rajajinagar, Avalahalli, and Dabaspet KIADB), with **5 regional leads (10.4%)** in Belagavi (3) and Davanagere (2).
- **Evidence Integrity:** Zero duplicate records, 95.8% phone availability, 87.5% management leadership identified in directory blocks, and all candidate claims classified into `DIRECTORY_VERIFIED`, `INTERVIEW_REQUIRED`, and `PRODUCT_PROPOSAL`.
- **Zero Real Data Leakage:** In accordance with instructions, **no businesses have been contacted**, and **no real company information has been added to application demo data or source code**.

### 2.3 Resolution of 161.70 MT vs. 164.20 MT Discrepancy:
- **161.70 Million Tonnes** is the official **Finished Steel Production** in India for FY 2025–26 (Source: JPC, Table 1 & Table 2).
- **164.20 Million Tonnes** is the official **Finished Steel Consumption** in India for FY 2025–26 (Source: JPC, Table 2).
- **Economic Explanation:** In FY 2025–26, domestic finished steel consumption exceeded domestic finished steel production by **2.50 Million Tonnes**. This net gap was satisfied through steel inventory changes and import-export trade movements.

### 2.4 Methodological Limitation:
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Macroeconomic Aggregate Non-Inference Rule:**  
> National production (161.70 MT) and consumption (164.20 MT) data describe macro commodity scale and industrial demand at the sovereign level. **They cannot and must not be used to infer MSME-level steel purchase prices, scrap generation percentages, factory operational costs, or profit margins.** MSME procurement operates under local distributor spot terms (retail spreads, freight surcharges, and credit premiums) that require empirical field interview confirmation.
